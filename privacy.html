Privacy Policy

Design+ by Kartikey · Last updated: 3 August 2026
Short version: Design+ stores your notes on your own device. There is no server. We never see, collect, or transmit your notes.

1. Who we are

Design+ by Kartikey is a personal, non-commercial note-taking app made by an individual developer. Contact: dailymovieshd@gmail.com

2. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no cookies used for tracking. Your notes are saved in your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB) and never leave your device unless you choose to export or back them up yourself.

3. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app requests a single permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This permission allows the app to create and update only the backup file it creates itself. It does not allow the app to read, list, open, modify or delete any other file in your Google Drive.

  • Backup data travels directly from your device to your own Google Drive account.
  • The developer has no access to your Google account, your files, or your backups.
  • Your Google sign-in token is stored only in your device's browser and is never transmitted to the developer or any third party.
  • The app does not read your name, email address, contacts or profile.

You can disconnect at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

4. Use of Google user data

Design+'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used solely to provide the backup and restore feature requested by the user. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with third parties.

5. Feedback form (optional)

The app has an optional Feedback button. It is used only if you choose to write a message and press Send.

  • Your message is delivered to the developer's email through Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service. Web3Forms does not store your message after delivery.
  • Along with your message, the app sends your browser/device identification string, the number of notes stored, and the current time. This helps reproduce bugs.
  • The contents of your notes are never sent.
  • Your email address is sent only if you type it in yourself, and is used only to reply to you.
  • If you never press Send, nothing is transmitted.

6. Data retention and deletion

Notes stay on your device until you delete them, or until you clear the app's storage from Android Settings. Backup files stay in your Google Drive until you delete them yourself. The developer stores no copy of anything and therefore has nothing to retain or delete.

7. Children

The app is intended for general study use and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.
